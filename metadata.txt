    Three processing modes in one module:
      • BT       — Brightness Temperature (Band 10)
      • LST      — Land Surface Temperature (B4+B5+B10, NDVI-based emissivity)
      • LST+UHI  — LST + UTFVI + UHI difference + NDBI + CSV statistics

    Support: Landsat 8 (LC08) / Landsat 9 (LC09), Collection 2, Level-1.
    Requirements: numpy, GDAL (included in a standard QGIS installation).

    Три режима в одном модуле:

      • LST+UHI  — LST + UTFVI + UHI diff + NDBI + CSV-статистика

    Поддержка: Landsat 8 (LC08) / Landsat 9 (LC09), Collection 2, Level-1.
    Требует: numpy, GDAL (входят в стандартную установку QGIS).
